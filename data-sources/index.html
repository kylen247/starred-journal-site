Data sources and licenses

Some restaurant, place and map information in Starred Journal comes from open data. We are grateful to these projects.

  • Map and place data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). openstreetmap.org/copyright
  • Facts about places and people: Wikidata, released under CC0. wikidata.org
  • Photos: from Wikimedia Commons. Each photo shows its author and license (for example CC BY-SA 4.0). Tap a photo's credit for details. commons.wikimedia.org
  • Places data: Overture Maps Foundation, under the CDLA Permissive 2.0 license. overturemaps.org
  • Places data: Foursquare Open Source Places, under the Apache License 2.0, © Foursquare Labs, Inc. opensource.foursquare.com/os-places
  • Awards and distinctions are facts from the awarding organizations and are used under the disclaimer below. Starred Journal is not affiliated with them.
