This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Starred Journal app for iPhone, why, who handles it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language. If something is unclear, email us.

1. The short version

  • You sign in with your email. We send you a one-time code. We don't use passwords (one demo account used by Apple's App Review is the only exception).
  • We store your journal entries, photos, profile and submissions so the app can work. Your photos are kept in a private storage area, stripped of hidden location and camera data before they leave your phone, and are visible only to you unless you choose to share them or make them public.
  • The AI feature is optional and off until you say yes. If you turn it on, we send the visit facts you chose to xAI.
  • We read your location on your phone only to show you nearby places. We don't store it or send it to our servers or anyone else.
  • We don't sell your information. We don't run ads. We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account inside the app.

2. Information we collect

You give us:

  • Email address, to create your account and send sign-in codes and important notices.
  • Profile details you choose to add: display name and username.
  • Journal content: visit dates, restaurant names, notes, ratings, tasting details, and photos you attach.
  • Photos you upload: when you attach a photo to a visit, the app uploads a copy to our cloud storage so it is available on your devices. Videos stay on your phone and are not uploaded. Before the upload, the app removes the photo's hidden metadata (EXIF data), including GPS location, the date and time it was taken, and camera details. We never receive that metadata. Photos are stored in a private storage bucket at Supabase. They are visible only to you unless you share the visit they belong to on a public (or followers-only) passport. Photos attached to visits you share publicly are visible to anyone who can see that visit on your public profile. Photos on visits you keep private are never shown to anyone else. You can unshare or delete them at any time. We do not use your photos to train AI and we do not send them to xAI.
  • Submissions: restaurants and chefs you suggest, and dining trails you create or share.
  • Reports, blocks and messages to us, including emails to kyle.igamcompany@gmail.com.
  • Your AI consent choice and when you made it.

We collect automatically:

  • Basic technical records from our service providers, such as your IP address, device type, app version, and error logs, used to run and secure the service.
  • A random user ID, so your data stays linked to your account.

What we do not collect:

  • Precise or approximate location. With your permission, the app uses your phone's location to sort places near you. That happens on your phone. The location isn't saved to your account or sent to our servers. Photo location data is removed from your photos before upload (see "Photos you upload" above).
  • Contacts, advertising identifiers, payment cards, or health information. Please don't put sensitive details such as health conditions, allergies, religion, or sexual orientation in your journal or profile.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: sign you in, store and show your journal, run your profile, and show shared items.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, enforce our Terms, block abuse, prevent fraud, and meet legal duties.
  • To send service messages: sign-in codes, security and account notices, changes to our policies, and replies to you. We don't send marketing email.
  • To improve the app: fix bugs, and understand what works, using aggregate technical information.
  • To provide the optional AI feature (only with your consent), described in section 5.
  • To comply with the law, and to establish or defend legal claims.

If you are in a place with a legal-basis rule such as the EU or UK: we rely on contract (to provide the app), consent (the AI feature, which you can withdraw), legitimate interests (safety, security, improving the app) and legal obligation. The app is currently offered in the United States only.

Other users and the public. Your passport (public profile) is private by default, and so is your whole journal: until you choose Followers or Public in your profile, other people see nothing but your name. If you choose Public, anyone who uses the app can view your passport, including people who are not signed in, and it may be copied or screenshotted by others. If you choose Followers, only people who follow you can. A visible passport shows your display name and handle, your dining stats and showcase, and all of the visits you have logged (the restaurant, date, dishes and wines, and prices unless you hide them), not only the ones you shared. If you do not want your visits seen, keep your passport Private. Your private notes are never shown. Photos attached to visits you share publicly are visible to anyone who can see that visit on your public profile; you can unshare or delete them anytime. Photos on visits you keep private are never shown to anyone else. Shared trails, and restaurant or chef submissions you share, can be seen by anyone who uses the app.

Legal and safety. We may share information if the law requires it (a valid legal request), to enforce our Terms, or to protect people from harm. We may report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as the law requires.

Business changes. If the app moves to a company or is sold, your information would move with it, and the promises in this policy would continue to apply unless you are told about a change and given choices.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context advertising.

5. The optional AI feature (xAI Grok)

The AI feature can write a short summary or suggestions from a visit's facts. It is off by default. The first time you try it, we show a prompt asking for your permission. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • What we send to xAI: the post facts you chose to include (the restaurant's name and cuisine, its awards, the visit date, and the dishes, wines and notes you switch on for that post). We do not send your photos, email address, or location. We send a scrambled version of your user ID so xAI can detect abuse without knowing who you are.
  • Why: to get the AI result back to you.
  • What xAI does with it: xAI acts as our service provider. Under its current terms for the API, xAI keeps requests for up to 30 days to check for abuse and then deletes them, and does not use them to train its models by default. These terms are xAI's and may change. [TODO: decide whether to use xAI's US-only endpoint, then say so here]
  • Your choices: you can turn the feature off at any time in Settings > Account > AI, and we stop sending data immediately. Withdrawing consent does not affect past requests, which xAI deletes on its own schedule. If you delete your account, we delete our record of your AI use.
  • Accuracy: AI results can be wrong or odd. They aren't professional advice and aren't facts. Review them before you save or share them.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: you can see and edit your profile and journal in the app.
  • Delete your account: Settings > Account > Delete account. It removes your account and the personal data attached to it, as described in section 6. You can also email kyle.igamcompany@gmail.com; you do not need the app to ask us.
  • Export your data: email us and we'll send you a copy of your journal in a common format within 45 days.
  • Location: change in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. Starred Journal works without it.
  • Photos and camera: change what the app can access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos. You can stop sharing a visit's photos, or delete the visit with its photos, in the app at any time.
  • AI consent: change in Settings > Account > AI.
  • Email: we only send service messages.
  • Do Not Track: we do not track you across other websites or apps, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we do not respond to browser Do Not Track signals differently.

If you live in California, Oklahoma, or another US state with a privacy law, you may have the right to know what we collect, get a copy, correct or delete it, and appeal a refusal. We give these choices to all US users. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. Email kyle.igamcompany@gmail.com. We'll reply within 45 days. We may need to verify it's you by sending a code to your account email.

If you are in the EU, UK or similar: you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, move your data, and complain to your data-protection authority. [TODO: remove this paragraph if the app stays US-only (recommended at launch)]

8. Children

Starred Journal is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under that age. If we learn that we have, we'll delete the account and its information. If you think a minor is using the app, email kyle.igamcompany@gmail.com.

Age check on iPhone. In places where the law requires it (such as Texas), before you create an account the app asks Apple for your age range (for example, "18 or older"), not your birthday. Apple shows you a sheet and you choose whether to share. If you are under 18, or you decline where the law requires the check, we can't create an account. We use the answer once, on your phone, to decide whether to continue. We do not store it or send it to our servers or anyone else.

9. Security

We use reputable providers and protect data in transit with encryption (HTTPS) and with access controls. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.
