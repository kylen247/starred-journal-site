Common questions

I did not get my sign-in code. Check spam, wait a minute and request a new code. Type the code into the app; there is no link to tap.

How do I delete my account? Settings, Account, Delete account. You type DELETE and enter a code we email you. It removes your account and the personal data tied to it. You can also email us.

How do I report or block someone? Open their profile, trail or entry and tap Report or Block. Manage blocked accounts in Settings. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Is my journal private? Yes by default. Only you can see it unless you set your passport to followers or public.

A restaurant's details are wrong. Email us the restaurant name and what to fix.
